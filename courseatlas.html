What Course Atlas is, and the problem it is for

A training coordinator's question is rarely just which course. It is which course, in which language, on a date this worker is free, before their certificate expires. Answering it today means opening each training provider's website in turn, or sending the same message to five of them and waiting.

Course Atlas gathers published course dates from Singapore training providers into one searchable calendar. You can see every session of a course across providers on one month view, filter it down to the language and the days that work, and shortlist the ones worth asking about.

It is deliberately honest about what it is: a snapshot of published information, with every listing linked back to its source. It does not take bookings or payment. In the November 2026 snapshot it held 2,753 listings from 14 providers.

What it does, function by function

Four viewsCalendar, list, map, coverage
A month, week or agenda calendar of sessions; a sortable course list; a map of training centres; and a provider coverage view that shows which providers are included.
FiltersNarrow to what is usable
Filter by course type (fresh or full course against recertification), industry scope, provider, teaching language, training mode, seat availability, published fee ceiling and days of the week. Common courses have one-tap chips.
Date distributionSee the busy and quiet days
A chart shows how published sessions are spread across the month, which makes it easy to spot the dates with the most choice.
Shortlist and booking requestKeep the candidates together
Courses can be shortlisted and gathered into a booking request list, ready to take to the provider.
ExportTake it with you
The current selection exports to CSV, to an ICS calendar file, or to JSON.
Source-linked listingsCheck it at the source
Each listing links to the provider's own page, shows explicit attendance dates, and labels seat counts as saved observations with the date they were seen.
LanguagesInterface in three languages
The interface is available in English, Simplified Chinese and Traditional Chinese, and courses can be filtered by their teaching language.

Who it is for. Training coordinators, employers and workers in Singapore who book safety and trade courses often and across more than one provider.

Where Course Atlas is stronger

  • One view across providers. The comparison that used to take several browser tabs happens on one calendar.
  • It starts from the date. It answers the question coordinators really have, which is when, not just what.
  • Language is a filter. Finding a session in the right teaching language takes one click, not five enquiries.
  • You can take the result away. CSV, calendar and JSON exports fit into whatever you already use.
  • It shows its working. Listings link to their source, and observations are labelled as observations.
