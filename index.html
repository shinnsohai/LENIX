Four products under one umbrella.

Each one has its own page with what it does, how it compares with the usual alternatives, and where it is stronger.

BuiltSync

Workforce OS for project sites

A worker app and an admin console for project sites that replace paper timesheets and scattered spreadsheets. Built around Singapore work permits, CPF and MOM-issued certificates.

  • Geofenced, face-verified check-in and check-out
  • Payslips that workers confirm or dispute in the app
  • Permit and certificate expiries tracked with reminders
How BuiltSync works Try the interactive demo

EZJOB

Skilled trades and technical talent matching

A hiring platform built for construction, electrical and heavy industry. Workers carry a verified Digital Skill Passport; employers get ranked candidates instead of keyword hits.

  • Digital Skill Passport with video skill demonstration
  • AI Job Studio drafts a job specification from a trade title
  • Free for workers
How EZJOB works Open EZJOB

Course Atlas

Singapore training course calendar

A searchable calendar of published training courses in Singapore, for coordinators, employers and workers who need to compare dates, providers, languages and fees in one view.

  • Calendar, list, map and provider-coverage views
  • Filter by course type, language, fee and day of week
  • Export a shortlist to CSV or your calendar
How Course Atlas works Open Course Atlas

Smart Site

RFID control for workers, vehicles and materials

RFID control for construction-stage sites, where thousands of workers pass gates that have no permanent access control yet. One tag per worker, vehicle and item.

  • Gate checks by handheld, with photo and status on screen
  • Toolbox meeting headcount in one sweep
  • Live count per floor, and a missing list at muster
